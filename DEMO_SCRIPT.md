# VoiceDebt Demo Script

Target: 60–90 seconds.

## Shot 1 — the problem
Open the landing page.

Say:
> My friend sends voice notes like podcasts. The problem isn't listening — it's remembering every question, plan, promise, and tiny detail I meant to reply to.

Pause on:
**Your voice notes have outstanding debt.**

## Shot 2 — upload a real voice note
Drop in a real note from a friend.

Say:
> VoiceDebt transcribes the note with Whisper, then Gemma separates the story from the actual reply debt.

Let the processing state run briefly.

## Shot 3 — the core magic
On the result card, point out:
- **THE ACTUAL STORY**
- **YOU OWE THEM**
- **DO NOT FORGET**
- **SUGGESTED REPLY**

Say:
> Instead of summarizing everything into mush, it asks a more useful question: what do I personally owe this person?

Copy the reply.

## Shot 4 — prove directionality
Upload or show the Sales rep refund note.

Say:
> This mattered in testing. The sender promised me a refund, so VoiceDebt correctly says I owe them nothing. Their promise becomes context, not my task.

Show:
**NO DEBT**
and the refund timing under **DO NOT FORGET**.

## Shot 5 — inbox
Open Reply Debt.

Say:
> Only notes with actual obligations enter the debt queue. Informational notes don't inflate the inbox.

Show the outstanding people and obligation count.

## Shot 6 — clear the debt
Open one actionable note and click **Clear debt**.

Say:
> Reply sent. Debt cleared.

End on the landing page or empty inbox.

## Final line
> VoiceDebt is built with open models because voice notes are personal, messy, and worth understanding on our terms.
