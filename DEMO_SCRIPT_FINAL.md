# VoiceDebt — Final Demo Script

Target: 60–90 seconds. Keep the cursor moving and narration tight.

## 0:00–0:10 — Problem

Open the landing page.

Say:

> My friend sends voice notes like podcasts. The problem isn't listening — it's remembering the three questions, one promise, and random detail I meant to reply to.

Pause briefly on:

**Your voice notes have outstanding debt.**

## 0:10–0:25 — Upload

Drop in one real friend voice note with at least one direct ask.

Say:

> VoiceDebt transcribes the note with Whisper, then Gemma separates what happened from what I actually owe the sender.

Let the processing animation show for a moment.

## 0:25–0:43 — The result

Scroll through:

- **THE ACTUAL STORY**
- **YOU OWE THEM**
- **DO NOT FORGET**
- **SUGGESTED REPLY**

Say:

> A summary isn't enough. I want to know what I personally need to answer, send, confirm, or remember.

Copy the reply.

## 0:43–0:58 — Directionality proof

Show the Sales rep refund result.

Say:

> Real testing caught the important edge case. This sales rep owes me a refund, so VoiceDebt correctly says NO DEBT. Their promise is context, not my task.

Show the refund timing under **DO NOT FORGET**.

## 0:58–1:10 — Inbox

Open Reply Debt.

Say:

> Only notes with actual obligations enter the queue. Advice and informational notes don't artificially increase the count.

Open one actionable note and click **Clear debt**.

## 1:10–1:18 — Close

End on the empty inbox or landing page.

Say:

> Whisper hears the note. Gemma figures out the debt. I finally remember what I meant to reply to.

On-screen links:
- voicedebt.onrender.com
- github.com/ShalyX/voicedebt

## Recording rules

- Do not expose a private real transcript that you do not have permission to show.
- Use the synthetic Amaka note for any sensitive portion.
- Keep browser zoom around 90–100%.
- Warm the Render service before recording.
- Hide bookmarks/personal tabs.
- Record one clean take rather than stitching many tiny clips.
